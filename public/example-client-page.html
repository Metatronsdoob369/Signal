Signal example client page

By Signal · Updated 9 September 2026

Signal is an embeddable audit pack that reads structured signals from a page on load and scores them deterministically. This page is intentionally structured so a real beacon can score SEO and AI-readiness signals without touching any other product.

Key takeaways

  • One script tag, one embed key, one dashboard.
  • Structured signals only: counts, booleans, and head metadata.
  • No cloud model on the ingest path.

What does the pack collect?

The pack collects head metadata such as the title, meta description, canonical link, and Open Graph tags, together with heading levels, paragraph and sentence counts, image and link counts, JSON-LD types with their property names, and browser performance timings. Body text is measured locally and only the numbers leave the page.

How do you verify Signal?

Open this page with a valid embed key query string, then refresh the dashboard for that site. A new audit row appears with scores and findings, and the experiments board seeds a title variant for this path. That single round trip proves the wedge works end to end.

Why does structured data matter for AI readiness?

Structured data is a machine-readable statement of what a page is and who stands behind it. Answer engines lean on it to decide whether a page can be quoted, so an Organization node with sameAs links and a page-type node such as FAQPage or Article move the AI-readiness dimensions more than any other single change.
